Only BasicAgent came across, and BasicAgent has NO longitudinal following model
at all: run_step tests "is anything inside 5 + speed metres" and, if so, calls
add_emergency_stop -- zero throttle, full brake. On/off, with no gap, headway or
relative-speed term. Measured on the MLK U-turn corridor: 27 impacts in 3189 s,
11 of them with the hazard flagged continuously for the preceding 2 s, closing to
3.0 m. The agent saw them and had no way to respond but to slam on.

If an application needs to drive in traffic rather than follow a path, vendor
those two and subclass BehaviorAgent. The FIXS sensing overrides carry across
unchanged -- BehaviorAgent inherits _vehicle_obstacle_detected from BasicAgent.
